## After the final whistle

Iraola's Liverpool couldn't find the response their home crowd wanted. After
being pegged back for a stoppage-time draw at Newcastle on the opening
weekend, they found themselves chasing the game again at Anfield — twice.
Dan Ndoye's composed 24th-minute finish gave Forest a deserved lead, and the
half-time numbers (0.25 xG for Liverpool against Forest's 1.29, despite 63%
possession) told the real story: plenty of the ball, precious little
penetration.

The second half was a different watch. Alexander Isak scored his first goal
at Anfield in the 59th minute. The lead didn't last: Alisson's challenge on
Neco Williams inside the box gave away a penalty, and Morgan Gibbs-White
converted calmly to restore Forest's advantage. Eight minutes from time,
Florian Wirtz picked out Victor Munoz, whose clean right-footed strike beat
the keeper and spared Anfield's blushes.

## The good

- Character in the second half — coming back from behind twice isn't easy
- The Wirtz-Munoz combination in the closing stages

## The bad

- A timid, disorganized first half — that 0.25 to 1.29 xG gap wasn't an accident
- Alisson's rashness in the box cost a second goal
- Two draws in two games leaves Iraola still searching for his first win

It was a mixed afternoon for Alisson — the challenge before the penalty was
reckless, though he read the rest of Forest's shots well enough. Van Dijk
tried to organize a defense that was regularly stretched by Forest's
transitions. Wirtz's influence in the final 20 minutes changed the game; the
assist was just the visible part of it. And Munoz — the £34.5m summer
signing is already delivering on the price tag.

Next up is Portman Road on September 4. The clock is ticking on Iraola's
first win.
